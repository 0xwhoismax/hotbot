02

Why we built Hot Bot

We got tired of doing the same checks, waiting on entries, and watching every bag ourselves. You can have a solid trading plan and still spend your whole day carrying it out.

So we built the team we wanted in the trenches. Give Hot Bot your plan and let it handle the repeated work while you find another setup, trade manually, or get on with your day.

03

A few clicks. Your own setup.

A Strategy is your trading plan: which coins to watch, what to check, when to buy, and when to sell.

Start with a style:

  • Clips: New coins, quick moves, fast exits when a move turns.
  • Balanced: Coins already moving, with volume behind them.
  • Degen: Brand-new coins with big swings in both directions.

Set your budget, the most the team can spend on one coin, and how many coins it can hold. Choose an exit template. Review the plan in plain words and start.

Want to get specific? Describe your own setup. Customize what the team looks for, what it checks, how it enters, and how it gets out. Buy when a coin passes your checks or wait for a dip.

Choose approval before each buy, or automatic buys within your limits where supported. From there, your team follows the plan in the background. No code required.

04

Take profits. Have a plan.

Set your exit plan before you get in. Let the team follow it.

Choose a take-profit template or set your own numbers. Sell some after a rise, leave the rest running, and set a stop if the price falls too far.

A trailing stop follows the price up and tells the team to try to sell when it drops by the amount you chose. TraderBot keeps managing the coins left after a partial sale.

TraderBot can exit sooner, but it cannot loosen your exit rules to hold longer.

05

Everyone has a job

Meet your team. Each bot has a job, and Hot Bot keeps them working together.

ResearchBot checks the setup. Finds coins that fit your Strategy and checks holders, liquidity, and trading activity.

SniperBot watches the entry. Uses the research and waits for your buying condition. You can keep looking at other setups.

TraderBot manages the bag. Handles buys, reviews your positions, and follows your take-profit and exit rules.

LaunchBot prepares your launch. Launching your own coin? It brings the token settings, wallet, and funding checks together for you to review and approve before launch.

Hot Bot keeps the work moving. Connects the team, carries the plan forward, and shows you what happened or needs your attention.

Your team has arrived.

06

Give it a Strategy. Watch the work.

Say your plan is to check new coins, buy when your rules pass, and take some profit after a rise. Here's how the team carries it out.

ResearchBot skips a coin because a few wallets own too much of it. Another passes your checks, and the findings go to SniperBot to watch for your entry.

When your entry condition hits, the team buys within your permissions. TraderBot follows your exit plan while ResearchBot checks the next coin. Your budget and position limits still apply.

Come back and see what the team checked, skipped, bought, and sold. Open a decision to see why. The market decides the outcome; your team handles the work.

That's what you're setting up in those few clicks: your plan, running through a team that works together.
